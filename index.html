Spendlane: Budget & Expenses

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: Spendlane keeps your financial records on your phone. It has no accounts and no servers that store your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and it sends anonymous usage events to Firebase Analytics, which you can turn off at any time. Where consent is required, it stays off until you opt in. You can change your ad and analytics choices at any time.

1. Who we are

Spendlane is developed by Digvijay Katkar. You can contact us at thehe3742@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The following information is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Accounts, categories, transactions, budgets, and recurring payment rules that you enter.
  • Settings such as currency, theme, app lock, and your choices for analytics and ads.
  • Records of when ads were last shown, so the app can limit how often they appear.

We do not receive, copy, or store this information on any server. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you choose Delete all local data in Settings.

3. Backup files and exports

You can save a full backup, or export your transactions or accounts as CSV files. These files are saved where you choose on your device. Spendlane does not upload them. If you share a backup file, you decide who receives it. Keep backup files safe, because they contain your financial records. Backup and CSV files are not encrypted, so anyone who can open the file can read its contents.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spendlane shows banner and full-screen ads served by Google AdMob, which is part of Google. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android settings;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app and device information, such as app version, device model, and language;
  • information about ads you see and tap.

Spendlane does not send your transactions, notes, account names, or category names to Google or to any advertiser. Ad requests use no custom targeting based on your financial records.

Spendlane's AdMob identifiers are:

  • App ID: ca-app-pub-7681094000019467~7232739638
  • Banner ad unit: ca-app-pub-7681094000019467/7296933792
  • Interstitial ad unit: ca-app-pub-7681094000019467/6598095641

Legal basis (EEA and UK): consent, given through the consent form in section 5.

To review or reset your advertising ID, go to your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Google → Ads), then choose Delete advertising ID. You can also manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

How Google uses this information is described in the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your consent and ad choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where the law requires it, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before it requests ads. Ads are not requested until consent allows them.

Google's User Messaging Platform stores your consent choice, so that the form is not shown again unnecessarily.

  • In the United States, where state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) apply, the form offers the choices those laws require. To opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising, use the Ad privacy choices option in the app, or the link below.
  • Do Not Sell or Share my personal information: adssettings.google.com (ad personalization). This page is controlled by Google.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Data and privacy → Ad privacy choices. This option appears wherever the law requires it.
  • You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your Android settings.

6. Usage analytics (you can turn it off)

Where the law does not require consent (for example, in the United States), analytics is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Data and privacy → Usage analytics. Where the law requires consent (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), analytics stays off until you turn it on in the same place. If the app cannot determine whether consent is required, analytics also stays off until you turn it on. While it is on, the app sends Google Firebase Analytics a small set of event names:

  • app_opened, when the app opens;
  • transaction_saved, with only the type of transaction (expense, income, or transfer).

Legal basis: consent where required, and your choice everywhere. You can turn analytics off at any time, and the events stop.

These events contain no amounts, notes, account names, or category names. Firebase also collects standard app and device information, such as an app instance identifier, app version, and device model. You can turn analytics off at any time, and the events stop. Firebase is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications, to remind you of recurring payments. Reminder text never includes amounts.
  • Network state, to know when a connection is available, so ads can load after it returns.
  • Advertising ID, required by the ads SDK on newer Android versions.
  • Biometrics (optional), to unlock the app. The app uses the system prompt and never sees fingerprint or face data.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or address.
  • We do not sync your financial records to a server.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, messages, or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Spendlane is not a financial service. It does not connect to your bank, hold or move money, lend, or process payments. It is a personal record-keeping tool that you fill in yourself.

9. Security

Your records stay in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects. The app does not add a separate encryption layer on top of the device's own protection, so keep your phone locked and turn on the app lock for extra protection. Requests made by the ads and analytics SDKs use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it, delete all local data in the app, or uninstall the app.

Data held by Google for ads and analytics is kept for the periods set by Google. For the retention of Firebase Analytics data, see Firebase privacy and data retention. You can stop new collection at any time by turning analytics off in the app, or your ad choices off as in section 4.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to some processing. Most of your financial records are on your device, so you can view, edit, export, or delete them in the app at any time. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. For data held by Google, use the choices in section 5 and your Google account settings. To make another request, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within the time the law requires.

12. Children

Spendlane is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will act on it.

13. International use

Google may process ad and analytics data in the United States and other countries, including countries outside the EEA and UK. For transfers from the EEA and UK, Google relies on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and the UK, as described in its privacy policy. Your financial records stay on your device and do not move across borders through Spendlane.
